I have looked at the definition of precision and recall so many god-damn times, that I thought, I surely remember them by now.
Well, I don't.
Today was yet another day, that I saw a precision value and asked myself: "How is precision defined again?"
However, today, something was different. Of course, I could not remember the definition. But! I could derive it from a mental picture that I constructed last time I had the exact same problem.
So, I decided to create a nice summary of this mental picture that I can look up the next time I am having a hard time remembering the metrics. Which will be the very next time I will think about them, I am sure of that.
